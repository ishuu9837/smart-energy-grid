## Simulation
Website-only one-hour energy balance illustration: adjustable demand (MW), renewable availability (MW), and demand shift (%). Shifted demand = demand * (1-shift/100), renewable used = min(renewables, shifted demand), conventional supply = max(shifted demand-renewables,0), surplus = max(renewables-shifted demand,0). Shifted energy deferred beyond this illustrated hour. Assume lossless instantaneous balancing, no battery model, no ramp limits, no network constraints or forecasts. All values synthetic. No claimed connection to real meters or implemented AI.
